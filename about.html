About The Sol

The Sol is premier news source of Mars, providing colonists with all the information they need to not only survive but thrive during their stay on our red planet. Our team of top tier journalists work around the clock to provide you with everything as it happens, from the politics here and on Earth, to our emerging pop culture, to even local highlights from around the biosphere.

The Sol is part of Group 4's Junior Interdisciplinary Project at the California Academy of Mathematics and Science. This site is licensed under the MIT License.
